# The 12-Week Mosquito Season Tracker™

## The Weekly Rhythm That Makes Mosquito-Proofing Automatic

**Why this file exists:** Systems fail not because they're wrong, but because they're
forgotten — the busy week, the travel, the "we'll do it this weekend" that becomes
next month. This tracker turns the BiteProof™ 4-Ring Defense™ into a **10-minute
weekly habit** with clear jobs, a simple scorecard, and a family duty roster.
Print one copy per season (or keep it on your phone) and run it like a game:
**every week you complete the loop, your property gets harder for mosquitoes to live on.**

**How to use it:**
1. Start the week you buy the system (Week 1 = your launch week, with the 72-hour
   sequence from the Core Guide).
2. Every week, one person (the "Roster Lead") runs the 10-minute loop and checks
   the boxes. Rotate the lead weekly.
3. Fill in the bite log and the scorecard. Watch the trend — it's deeply satisfying.
4. After any heavy rain, add the 10-Minute Rain Sweep (from Tool #3) — it resets
   the clock on rain-created breeding.

## The Weekly 10-Minute Loop (every week, rain or shine)

**Monday (or your chosen day), 10 minutes:**

- [ ] **Zone 1 sweep (3 min):** dump saucers, buckets, toys, lids; shake tarps and
      covers; scrub + refill the bird bath (2×/week if you can)
- [ ] **Zone 2 & 3 quick pass (3 min):** check low spots, hoses, storage items;
      confirm nothing new is holding water
- [ ] **Zone 4 check (2 min):** dunks fresh? Rain barrel sealed? Pond moving?
      Pool circulating? (Full details in Tool #3)
- [ ] **Harbor trim (2 min):** grass and shrubs near seating still short? Deck
      corners clear?
- [ ] **Repellent stock check (30 sec):** bottles full? Wipes fresh? Kids' versions
      current? Permethrin clothes still in the rotation?
- [ ] **Intel check (30 sec):** county mosquito report — any new virus activity
      near you? (If yes → move up a gear per the Core Guide escalation rules.)

**Done. That's the week.** Ten minutes. No drama.

## Week-by-Week Detail

### Week 1 — Launch Week
- [ ] Core Guide read (20 min)
- [ ] Full 15-Minute Yard Audit (Tool #2) completed
- [ ] Full Standing-Water Kill List executed (Tool #3)
- [ ] Repellents + BTI dunks + permethrin purchased
- [ ] Red-Flag Card read by the adults (Tool #6)
- [ ] Tracker set up; duty roster assigned

### Week 2 — Barrier Week
- [ ] Screen audit + all repairs done (Tool #5)
- [ ] Door sweeps + weatherstripping checked
- [ ] Utility penetrations sealed
- [ ] Fans placed in seating areas + entryways
- [ ] Dusk Protocol posted and explained to the kids

### Week 3 — Skin Week
- [ ] Repellent Cheat Sheet printed + posted (Tool #4)
- [ ] Every family member's "scenario card" chosen
- [ ] Every-Outing Go-Bag packed (Tool #7)
- [ ] Permethrin treatment done on a set of outdoor clothes
- [ ] Family drill: everyone demonstrates correct application (ankles! neck!)

### Week 4 — Outing Week
- [ ] Outing & Travel Playbook reviewed (Tool #7)
- [ ] Camping/cookout/travel plans mapped to the playbooks
- [ ] Travel notices checked if a trip is coming (CDC site)
- [ ] Go-bag taken on its first real outing

### Weeks 5–12 — Maintenance Mode
- [ ] Weekly 10-minute loop, every week
- [ ] Rain sweeps within 48h of every heavy storm
- [ ] Month 2: quick re-audit (new items? new low spots?)
- [ ] Month 3: mid-season barrier re-check (screens still tight?)
- [ ] Peak season (your county's virus window): escalation gear per Core Guide
- [ ] Fall transition: gutters cleaned, leaves cleared from low spots, water
      features winterized

## The Rain Reset Rule

Rain is the system's kryptonite — it creates new nurseries overnight. **The rule:**
within 48 hours of any heavy rain (enough to leave puddles), someone runs the
10-Minute Rain Sweep from Tool #3. That's it. Do this one thing consistently and
you've eliminated the #1 reason mosquito pressure returns mid-season.

## The Escalation Gear (when your county reports virus activity)

Normal weeks = the standard loop. The week your county health department reports
West Nile (or EEE) activity in your area, or an outbreak is declared nearby:

- [ ] **Gear up Ring 1:** full repellent protocol for everyone, all day; long
      sleeves/pants at dawn & dusk; permethrin clothes on rotation
- [ ] **Gear up Ring 3:** dusk = doors closed, blinds drawn, indoor evenings
      (or fully screened/fanned outdoor zones only)
- [ ] **Gear up Ring 2:** extra rain sweep; treat every permanent-water source;
      consider larvicide on any neighbor-visible shared drainage
- [ ] **Watch Days 2–14** after any exposure (Red-Flag Card, Tool #6)
- [ ] Keep the rhythm: escalation is a gear shift, not a panic. The system still runs.

## The End-of-Season Review (Week 12 or first hard frost)

- [ ] **Count the wins:** bites prevented (compare Week 1–2 vs. Week 11–12),
      money saved (no gadgets that don't work), evenings enjoyed outside
- [ ] **Winterize:** drain + store hoses; cover or drain water features; clean
      gutters one last time; store the go-bag somewhere visible for next season
- [ ] **Note what you learned:** which zones bred most? Which family member is the
      mosquito magnet (yes, it's real — some people are genuinely more attractive
      to mosquitoes; they get the permethrin clothes first next year)?
- [ ] **File the tracker** with your household records. Next May, Week 1 takes
      20 minutes instead of 2 hours — because you've already done the audit once.

**The season-end truth:** you started the season in a record outbreak year, and you
finished it with a property that stops breeding mosquitoes, a family that applies
protection without being nagged, and a stack of evenings spent outside instead of
swatting. That's the whole point.
